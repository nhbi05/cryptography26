--- Summary ---
One changed character changes about half the bits of both outputs.
HMAC verifies the original message and rejects the modified one.
A plain hash can be recomputed by anyone, so it cannot prove who
sent the message. HMAC needs the secret key, so only a key holder
can produce a valid tag: it gives integrity AND authentication.
